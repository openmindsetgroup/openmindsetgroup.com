Last updated November 2026 · Open Mindset Group LLC

The short version

Your data stays on your phone. Lightarc has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. We never see your weight, your medications or your doses.

What Lightarc stores

Weigh-ins (weight, date, time and any note, plus body fat and muscle if an imported file had them), your settings and goal, milestones, and, if you use them, medications, doses, injection sites, on/off dates and side-effect notes. All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on this phone.

Health Connect

Health Connect is off until you turn it on, either when you first set up Lightarc or later in Settings. When it is on, Lightarc reads the weight records that other apps, such as your scale's app, saved in Health Connect, and keeps them with your other weigh-ins on this phone. Lightarc reads weight and nothing else, and never writes to Health Connect. This data is used only to show your weigh-ins and trend. It is never sent to us, never shared with or sold to anyone, and never used for advertising. You can turn Health Connect off in Settings or remove Lightarc's access in Health Connect at any time; weigh-ins already brought in stay on this phone until you delete your data.

Android Backup

If "Back up to your Google account" is on (it is by default), Android copies Lightarc's data into your Google account's backup, so a new phone or a reinstall can restore it. Google stores and encrypts that backup; we can't read it. You can turn it off in Settings; Android then replaces the backup copy at its next scheduled backup, usually within a day.

Export and import

Files you export go only where you choose to save them. Files you import are read on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Purchases

Google Play handles the Premium purchase. We don't receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Premium; that is the only reason it uses the internet.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Dose reminders show generic text unless you choose to show the medication's name.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything from this phone. Uninstalling Lightarc does the same. Turn off Android Backup first if you also want the backup copy gone.

Not a medical device

Lightarc does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and never gives medical advice. Talk to your prescriber about your medication.

Children

Lightarc is not directed at children under 13.

If you email us, we use your message only to reply. Questions: lightarc@openmindsetgroup.com
